# Pop & Dance Homies — dedupe and release selection

The brief contains 35 named concepts: 25 pop-video homages and 10 nostalgic
dance-floor loops.

All 35 were checked against the shipped 277-animation manifest by normalized
slug and loading text. None is already present in the production gallery.
`Napoleon-Dynamiting…` appeared in an earlier idea list, but it was not among
the 36 Movie Night assets actually produced, so it remains a valid new asset
for this release.

Initial release result: **35 requested, 35 unique, 35 selected**.
