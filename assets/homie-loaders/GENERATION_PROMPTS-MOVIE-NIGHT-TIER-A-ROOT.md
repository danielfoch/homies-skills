# Movie Night Tier A — root production prompt ledger

Generation mode: built-in `image_gen`, one call per asset. Every accepted render
was keyed locally, resized uniformly to preserve at least 60 px cell clearance,
recomposited on exact `#00ff00`, assembled as `0,1,2,3,2,1`, and passed the
mandatory full-size and 128 px strict review.

Shared production contract for every prompt:

- exact 1254×1254 borderless 2×2 sheet, four equal 627×627 cells;
- supplied Homie reference is the sole identity/style anchor;
- four meaningfully distinct, reversible signature-action poses;
- exact flat `#00ff00` background, outer edges, and both centre axes;
- no floor, shadow, gradient, scenery, logo, watermark, crop, duplicate limb,
  detached hand, changing prop, or generic repeated standing pose;
- at least 65 px requested cell padding; same camera, identity, scale and
  persistent props in all four cells.
